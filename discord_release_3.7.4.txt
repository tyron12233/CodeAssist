# 🚀 CodeAssist 3.7.4

A quick stability hotfix on top of 3.7.3.

**FIXES**
- Fixed a **crash when opening the AI assistant** on 3.7.2 and 3.7.3 — the chat panel could close the app the moment it appeared (a missing bundled resource). It now opens reliably.
- Fixed a rare **editor crash** while drawing selection and search highlights right after a fast edit.
- Improved **crash reporting** so hard-to-reach faults point at a real location, helping us fix the next batch faster.

Full changelog: https://github.com/tyron12233/CodeAssist/releases/tag/v3.7.4
